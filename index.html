    Notes on the archive

    A Minecraft title screen is not a video. It is a cube: four walls, a sky and a ground, photographed in game and stitched into panorama_0 through panorama_5. The menu pans slowly across it, so the horizon never ends.

    Each entry is dated to the update that introduced its panorama. Where a pack shipped in phases, or differs between editions, the note says so.
